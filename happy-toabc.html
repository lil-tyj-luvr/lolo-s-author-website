👑 A Happy Ending for TOABC

What if...

What if Lilith made a different choice ? What if the crown didn't have to be broken ? Do not read that if you haven't read toabc yet.

This ending never happened. But you paid for the fantasy.

You want an alternate universe ? Fine. Lilith came to Moonhill to get her revenge at the very beginning. There, she met Kayden, by accident. They formed an alliance, with this goal:
Kill Rhodar, Aletha, and Kael Corven. They spent months to made up a plan, and went to the castle. Kayden pretending to be a new rich noble coming to greet the king, and Lilith pretending
(or not) to be his wife. That way, they infiltrate the castle. After some weeks there (yeah, you gotta be patient in that situation), Lilith killed Rhodar & Aletha, while Kayden was supposed
to kill Kael. When she came back to their room, Lilith discovered that Kayden didn't kill Kael, but Eris, the prince's sister. They fight, of course, it was not planned AND Kael was still alive
to arrest them both. Kayden explains that if he lost his sister, Skye, Kael had to suffer the same tragedy.
Finally, Kael dies from lilith's hands, like it was always meant to be. The two protagonists manage to escape the kingdom before being arrested. They infiltrated a truck full of Sylth slaves
that were being saved by Kael, and sent to the Névandors. In the truck, Lilith meets Hayley, a young girl of no more than 15 years, who becomes her "bestfriend". Until "Hayley" announces to Lilith
that her full name is actually Lisa Hayley Collins. Also known as Lilith's daughter. When they arrived to the capital of the Névandors, Lilith finds a work where she can be with Lisa "Hayley" and
become a teacher. Kayden becomes a soldier, and illustrates for his talent as a spy/stalker.
And together, they all live happily ever after.

Is it better that way to you or do you still grieve Kael ?
